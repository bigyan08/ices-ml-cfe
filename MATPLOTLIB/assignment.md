# Matplotlib Practice Assignment

## Instructions:
Complete the following tasks to practice creating different types of plots with Matplotlib. Make sure to add appropriate titles, labels, and customization to make your plots clear and professional.

---

## **Task 1: Monthly Expenses Bar Chart**

Create a bar chart showing your monthly expenses:
- **Categories:** Rent, Food, Transport, Entertainment
- **Amounts:** 800, 300, 150, 100
- Use a color of your choice
- Add title: "Monthly Budget Breakdown"
- Add appropriate labels (Category and Amount)

**Expected output:** A vertical bar chart with 4 bars representing each expense category.


## **Task 2: Age Distribution Histogram**

Create a histogram showing the age distribution in a classroom:
- **Ages:** 18, 19, 19, 20, 20, 20, 21, 21, 22, 22, 22, 22, 23, 23, 24, 25
- Use 5 bins to group the ages
- Color: 'lightcoral'
- Add black edge color to the bars (`edgecolor='black'`)
- Title: "Student Age Distribution"
- Labels: "Age" (x-axis) and "Number of Students" (y-axis)

**Expected output:** A histogram showing how students are distributed across different age groups.
